About the data. Prices, charts, ranges, volume and ratios come live from a public market data source and are usually delayed by about 15 minutes. Nothing on a company page is sample data: if a figure can’t be fetched it shows “—”, and if the feed is down the status says so. The top ribbon lists upcoming IPOs, dividends, splits, bonus and rights issues and earnings from public calendars (US markets, plus NSE when reachable). Items tagged “sample” are illustrative placeholders shown when those calendars can’t be reached, not official announcements. Always confirm with NSE, BSE or SEBI. Not investment advice.
